Hunt Lyman is looking forward to revisiting Charles Dickens' timeless holiday classic. Hunt taught English at the Hill School for 41 years and recently retired. He has played a number of roles with The Middleburg Players and other community acting groups, performing in Our Town, Guys and Dolls, The Music Man, Carousel, Kiss Me Kate, Of Mice and Men, The Cotton Patch Gospel, Working, Little Shop of Horrors, and a variety of original productions. He loved playing Scrooge in Tagragg’s previous productions of A Christmas Carol, and he is grateful to Erin Coulter for bringing community theater back to Middleburg.
