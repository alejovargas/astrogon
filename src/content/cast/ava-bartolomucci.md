Ava Bartolomucci is thrilled to return to Tagragg’s holiday tradition as the Spirit of Christmas Past. She is a student at Rollins College majoring in Communication Studies with a minor in Theatre Arts. Recently, Ava performed as a chorus dancer in Rollins’ production of Iphigenia in Aulis and just closed Let the People Sing, a student written production in which she helped bring the story to life as part of the ensemble. She would like to thank her family and friends for their constant encouragement and wishes everyone a wonderful holiday season!
