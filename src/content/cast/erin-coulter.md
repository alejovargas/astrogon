Erin Coulter is a theater director, producer, and educator based in Middleburg Virginia. With diverse and wide-ranging training in acting, singing, dancing, directing, and educating, Erin is known as an “actor’s director.” She believes in theater as a catalyst for change and connection; that through investigating, performing and ingesting stories, individuals and humanity can evolve.
