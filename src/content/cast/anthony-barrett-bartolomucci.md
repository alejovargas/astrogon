Anthony Barrett Bartolomucci is thrilled to make his TAGRAGG PRODUCTIONS debut in Beyond A Christmas Carol. An eighth-grade student at The Hill School in Middleburg, Virginia, Anthony recently appeared in Anything Goes as Sailor #1 - a role that further ignited his passion for the theater. Offstage, Anthony is an avid debater and public speaker, known for his confidence, poise, and ability to connect with an audience. He also enjoys playing soccer and basketball, and swimming. He extends his heartfelt thanks to his teachers, family, and friends for their unwavering support and encouragement. Anthony is grateful for the opportunity to help bring this twist on a Dickens’ classic and share it with you today.
