Alma Grace is thrilled to make her stage debut with Tagragg Productions in Beyond a Christmas Carol! A fourth grader at The Hill School, Alma Grace first caught the performing bug when she sang a duet with her father at the Hill School talent show. She loves reading, playing ice hockey, and inventing stories when she’s not rehearsing lines or humming tunes around the house. Alma Grace is grateful for the chance to work with such a wonderful cast and crew and would like to thank her family for their endless support, especially her little brother Judd for continually trying to tackle her when she is practicing her lines.
