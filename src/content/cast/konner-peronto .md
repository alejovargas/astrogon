Konner is a 7th grader at Hill School and is looking forward to playing Charlie in this production of Beyond a Christmas Carol. Previously, Konner has performed in three Hill School summer camps including Godspell (Judas), Legally Blonde (Aaron) as well as performing in Matilda (Escapologist) and Mary Poppins (Bert) outside of Hill School. Konner enjoys choreography, singing, performing, acting, and public speaking in general. His passion outside of theater is computer science, specifically programming and learning how computers work.
