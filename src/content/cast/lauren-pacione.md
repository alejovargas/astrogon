Lauren is thrilled to join TagRagg Productions in Beyond a Christmas Carol. While this is her first time in theatre, she brings extensive experience in public speaking, project management, and hospitality design. She is especially grateful to her daughter, Aspen, for inspiring her to take on this role, and to Erin for her thoughtful direction and encouragement to grow and share in the magic of Middleburg.
