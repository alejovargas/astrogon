Alice is thrilled to be returning to the stage for the first time in 15 years! After performing in school plays, dance, and singing showcases years ago, she rediscovered her love of performing through TAGRAGG’s improv class over the summer — an experience that unexpectedly led to this wonderful opportunity to join A Christmas Carol. Alice also enjoys writing and performing poetry and feels incredibly lucky to be part of such a talented and welcoming cast.
