Texas-raised, Berklee-graduated, music therapist, songwriter, anti-oppressive activist, and client advocate, Mowgli is grateful to be part of the TagRagg Production of Beyond a Christmas Carol. Mowgli’s theatre and performance history range from intimate living room concerts to The Kennedy Center stage and national television. For the last decade, Mowgli has spent the majority of his time in assisted living, hospitals, youth shelters, and homes, providing music therapy to clients from the NICU to end of life, striving to create more accessible & accepting communities through the arts!
