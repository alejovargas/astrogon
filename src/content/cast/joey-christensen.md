Joey is delighted to be making his debut with Tagragg Productions in Beyond a Christmas Carol! Favorite past credits include Seussical (JoJo), The Little Mermaid (Flounder), and Puffs (J. Finch Fletchley). Joey also performs with the South Loudoun Youth Chorale, where he enjoys learning challenging harmonies and performing for the community. A sixth grader at The Hill School in Middleburg, Virginia, Joey has a strong passion for storytelling through music and performance. When he’s not on stage, he loves swimming, reading books, sketching and drawing, playing games, and spending time with friends and family. Joey would like to thank his directors, teachers, and castmates for their guidance and encouragement, and his family for always supporting his love of the arts. He is thrilled to share this experience with the audience and hopes everyone enjoys the magic, laughter, and heart of this joyful, inspiring, and festive holiday season production.
