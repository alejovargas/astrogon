Isla excitedly accepted the offer to be a part of Beyond a Christmas Carol performing with the Tagraag Productions. This is Isla’s premier professional role. Previously, she performed in two productions, 33 Little Pigs and Giraffe’s Can’t Dance, at the Hill School in Middleburg, VA, as well as The Wizard of Oz at the Village Montessori School in Bluemont, VA. Isla is a 5th grade student at The Hill School where she enjoys reading, mythology, mathematics, science history. Isla speaks both French and English at home. In her free time Isla enjoys skiing, climbing, and is an avid equestrian. She also supports her mother with animal rescue and is currently raising five dogs, one scary cat, and two younger brothers, Lachlan (7) and Aidan (4).
